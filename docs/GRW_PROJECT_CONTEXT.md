# GRW Marketing — Project Context

Canonical business and product context for GRW Marketing. Future Cursor sessions must treat this file as the source of truth for positioning, compliance, architecture, and monetization decisions.

---

## Company Overview

GRW Marketing is building a long-term affiliate media company.

The objective is **not** to build a single affiliate website.

The objective is to build a **portfolio of trusted digital assets** that generate sustainable affiliate revenue through educational content.

Every decision should maximize **long-term enterprise value** rather than short-term profits.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become one of the most trusted independent resources in health optimization.

Future expansion may include:

- Peptides
- Longevity
- Hair
- Skin
- Biohacking
- Supplements
- Hormones
- Wellness technologies

The architecture should support future expansion without requiring a rebuild.

## Final Principle

Every design, SEO, development, and content decision should answer one question:

> Will this still be the right decision when GRW has 1,000 articles, multiple affiliate partners, and hundreds of thousands of monthly visitors?

---

## Publication Mindset (critical)

Build the website as if it will become a **real publication**, not an affiliate site.

That means:

- Every article has an author profile.
- Every article can have a reviewer profile.
- Editorial standards are public.
- Affiliate disclosures are transparent.
- Internal linking is intentional.
- Content is organized into topic hubs, not just categories.
- Commercial pages support educational content rather than dominating the site.
- The site earns trust before it asks for a click.

This mindset should guide decisions at every stage of development. If the site earns durable organic visibility over time, it should be because readers would have found it genuinely useful even if there were no affiliate links.
